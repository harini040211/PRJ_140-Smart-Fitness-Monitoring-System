How we calculate

BMI is weight divided by height squared. BMR uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Your daily need (TDEE) is BMR times an activity factor, then adjusted for your goal: 500 kcal less to lose weight, 400 more to gain, 250 more for muscle gain. You never type a calorie number yourself.
